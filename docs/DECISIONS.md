## Staff Clock In / Out built (16 Nov 2026)

The database decides who is allowed (head-office user only, via `my_billing_link()`) and works out the end date; the server then sets the Stripe subscription to end on that date (or removes the end date for "Keep my account"). If Stripe refuses, our records are put back, so the two always agree.
- **Free access codes** (migration 0032): staff make them on the Offers page (type "Free access"). Single-use, expiring, can be switched off. Using one opens the account with no Stripe step (`free_activate`, server key only), flags the company `free_access` and value £0. Free accounts are left out of the revenue figures. Intended for testing and for any invited free account.
- **Emails from the Stripe webhook** (only when the email service is set up, never blocks the webhook): a welcome/receipt after the first payment only; a failed-payment email to the head-office user plus a copy to info@tremcore.co.uk.
- **All emails sent by the Tremcore system use the branded template** (black and gold header, logo band, company footer, gold button for links, reply-to info@tremcore.co.uk): thank-you, offer, free access, "now open", welcome/receipt and failed payment. Sign-in emails (confirm address, reset password) come from Supabase and have their own templates in Supabase settings.

## Bulk upload of certificates, Level 1 (23 Nov 2026)
- Compliance page > "Bulk upload": choose up to 20 files (PDF, JPG, PNG, Word, 10 MB each). For PDFs with real text, the browser reads the text itself with plain rules (`web/public/app/certread.js`): what the certificate is (Gas, EICR, FRA, Legionella, insurance and so on), the dates, and the certificate or policy number. **No AI and no outside service reads the documents**, so it works without an Anthropic key.
- Suggestions only: the person checks every row (site, which check it files under or a new check, dates) and presses "Add all". Skipped or unreadable files are not stored. Photos, scans and Word files are not read (the person types the dates). Empty dates show as "No date", never green. A due date worked out from the usual repeat period is flagged.
- The PDF text reader (pdf.js 3.11.174) is loaded from the already-allowed jsdelivr CDN only when bulk upload is used. If it fails to load, rows simply come up as "Needs checking".
- Uses the existing database rules (documents and checks are limited to people who can edit that site), so no new migration. Level 2 and 3 clients are view-only and do not see the button. A Hub version for Tremcore staff is a later item.

## Certificate reminder emails (23 Nov 2026)
- **Level 1:** one summary email per person listing every item due within 30 days or overdue. Sent when an item newly reaches: 30 to 8 days before (once), 7 to 1 days before (once), the due date (once), then weekly while overdue, up to 8 chases per due date. A new due date starts the count again. Items with no date never trigger an email. Stopped or ended accounts get nothing.
- **Who:** the head-office user, plus site users who can update their site (their own sites only). View-only users are never emailed.
- **Levels 2 and 3:** one notice per item as it comes within 30 days: "Tremcore is managing the renewal and will be in touch", to the head-office user only. No overdue chasing.
- **How:** the database (`reminders_pending` / `reminders_mark`, migration 0033, service key only) decides and records, so nobody gets the same reminder twice. The server sends the emails once a day from 8am UK time when `REMINDERS_ON=1` (set on LIVE only). `POST /api/send-reminders` with `Authorization: Bearer <REMINDER_SECRET>` runs it by hand. Needs the Resend email set-up.

## Staff shifts and the shift report (24 Nov 2026)
- Part of the Staff Clock In / Out add-on (Level 1). Each person on a site's list gets weekly shifts: one start and finish time per weekday, Monday to Sunday; an empty day is a day off; a finish earlier than the start means the shift runs past midnight. Table `staff_shifts` (migration 0034), same permissions as the people list (site editors only, only where the add-on is on).
- **Shift report** (People page > "Open the shift report"): by day and person, with the shift, the clock-in and the clock-out, UK time, by week (previous/next), custom dates, one person or everyone, and a CSV download.
- **Colours (Aaron's choices):** start time is **red if even one minute late** (no grace period), **green if 5 or more minutes early**, plain otherwise. Finish time is **red if they left before the end of the shift**, plain otherwise. Words ("12 mins late", "left 30 mins early") are shown next to the colour so it never relies on colour alone. Times are compared to the minute (seconds ignored).
- A past shift day with no clock-in shows a muted "No clock-in recorded" row (not red, as it may be leave or sickness). Days off and days not yet over show nothing.
