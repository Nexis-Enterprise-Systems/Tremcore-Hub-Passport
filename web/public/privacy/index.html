How Tremcore uses your information

Who we are

Tremcore Ltd, company number 17507064, Goldfinch Grove, Rochester, Kent, ME3 8FX. Questions about your information: hello@tremcore.co.uk. [ICO REGISTRATION NUMBER TO BE ADDED.]

Two roles: your data and our own

Your records are yours. The compliance records, dates, notes, certificates, photographs, site details and the names of your own colleagues that you put into the passport belong to your company. For that information your company is the controller and Tremcore is your processor: we only handle it to run the service for you, on your instructions, as set out in our Terms and the data processing terms within them.

For our own account, billing and sales information (who you are, how to contact you, what you pay) Tremcore is the controller.

What we collect

When you enquire or create an account: your name, work email, phone number (if you give one), business name and the names and addresses of your sites. When you use the passport: the records, certificates and photographs you add, a record of changes made to them, and basic security information such as sign-in times. When you pay: payment details are collected and held by our payment provider, not by us .

Why we use it

To run your account and passport (to carry out our contract with you), to send reminders and service messages, to take payment and keep financial records (to meet our legal obligations), to protect the service (our legitimate interest in keeping it secure and fair), and to answer your enquiries. [LEGAL BASES TO BE CONFIRMED BY SOLICITOR.]

We do not share or sell your data

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or show it to other customers. Each company can only see its own records; this is enforced by the database itself. Tremcore staff only look at your records to support you. We only use a small number of service providers, strictly to run the service, under written contracts: hosting and database (Supabase), web hosting (Railway), email delivery ([EMAIL PROVIDER]) and payments ([STRIPE]). Where a provider is outside the UK we rely on approved transfer safeguards. [PROVIDERS, LOCATIONS AND SAFEGUARDS TO BE CONFIRMED.]

How long we keep it

While your account is open we keep your records. If you cancel, you can use the service until your notice ends; after that you can no longer sign in and your records, documents and photographs are permanently deleted shortly afterwards (within [14] days), except copies in secure backups, which expire on their normal cycle [BACKUP PERIOD TO BE CONFIRMED]. Billing records are kept for [6] years because the law requires it. Enquiries that do not become customers are deleted after [12] months.

Getting a copy of your data

Before your notice ends you can ask us for a copy of your records in a common format, free of charge. [EXPORT FEATURE TO BE BUILT.]

Security

Records are held in private storage. Access is checked by the database for every request, head-office users must use 2FA, and changes to records are logged.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, restrict or object to how we use it, or ask for it to be moved, by contacting hello@tremcore.co.uk. If your records are about people at your company or sites, we will pass requests about them to you as the controller. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).
