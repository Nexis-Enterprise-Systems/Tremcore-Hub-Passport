Questions

Before you ask

Something not covered here? Get in touch and we will answer it.

Is every check on the list a legal requirement?+

No. The list shows common checks for commercial premises. What applies to your property, and how often, depends on the premises, the equipment, current legislation and guidance, and your risk assessment. If you are unsure, ask us.

Can I keep using my own contractors?+

Yes. You stay in control. You choose your own contractors and record the results in your passport.

What happens if a date or certificate is missing?+

The check shows as “No date” instead of green, and it counts as not compliant in your percentage. Nothing is hidden to make a site look better than it is.

Can I manage more than one property?+

Yes. Several sites can sit under one company, and each property keeps its own passport and records.

Does it work on a phone?+

Yes. It is designed to work on phones, tablets and computers.

Can I see it before I decide?+

Watch the short preview on this site. The passport is simple to use, and we are adding short training videos so you can learn it at your own pace. If you have a question first, ask us.

How much does it cost?+

£14.99 a month for your first site, which includes a head-office user and one manager, and £7.99 a month for each extra site, which includes one more user. Pay yearly and save 15%. Prices exclude VAT.
