These terms are for businesses. By creating an account you confirm you are signing up for a business and that you are allowed to bind it to these terms.

1. What the service is

The Compliance Passport is a record-keeping tool. It helps you store certificates, track due dates and receive reminders. It does not carry out inspections, and nothing in it is legal, health and safety or fire-safety advice.

2. Your responsibilities

You are responsible for deciding which checks apply to your premises, arranging them, keeping your records accurate and up to date, and for the compliance of your premises. The checks and labels shown are examples and must be confirmed for your own premises and risk assessment. Reminders are a help, not a guarantee: please do not rely on them alone.

3. Your account

Keep your sign-in details and your authenticator app secure. 2FA is required for head-office users. Tell us straight away if you think someone else has access. You are responsible for what people you add to your account do with it.

4. Signing up

There is no free trial. You sign up and pay, and your account opens as soon as the payment is confirmed. We do not store your card details: payments are handled by our payment provider.

5. Price and payment

The price is £14.99 a month for your first location and £7.99 a month for each extra location, plus VAT. If you choose to pay yearly you get 15% off, paid in advance. [PRICES AND VAT TO BE CONFIRMED.] You choose monthly or yearly when you sign up. Payments are taken automatically by our payment provider on the same date each month (or year). Your first site includes a head-office user and one manager; each extra site includes one more user. Your subscription continues until you cancel. We will give you at least [30] days’ written notice before any price rise.

6. If a payment fails

We will tell the head-office user in the app and by email. If the payment is not made within 7 days, your account will be put on stop: nobody at your company will be able to open or see anything until the payment is made. Your data is not deleted just because of a failed payment, and the account opens again as soon as you pay what is owed. [HOW LONG A STOPPED ACCOUNT IS KEPT BEFORE CLOSURE TO BE CONFIRMED.]

7. Cancelling

Monthly plans: you can cancel at any time by giving us one month’s notice, using the Cancel button in the app or by writing to us. You keep full access and pay as normal until the notice ends.

Yearly plans (paid in advance): you can cancel at any time. The amount you have paid for the year is non-refundable, and you keep access until the end of the year you paid for (or the end of one month’s notice if later). [SOLICITOR TO CONFIRM THIS WORDING.]

When the notice ends: you will no longer be able to sign in, and your records, documents and photographs will be permanently deleted shortly afterwards. Please save anything you need before then; ask us if you would like a copy of your data. Nothing is kept for any other use.

All the records, documents and photographs you put in the passport belong to you. We use them only to provide the service to you. We do not sell them, share them with other customers or use them for marketing. Our service providers only handle them to run the service, under contract. How we handle personal data is set out in our Privacy notice.

For personal data in your records you are the controller and we are your processor. The detail is in a separate data processing agreement. [DPA TO BE DRAFTED BY SOLICITOR.]
