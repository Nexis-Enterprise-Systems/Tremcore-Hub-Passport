What it covers

The checks that keep a building safe, in one place

Each property’s passport holds the checks that apply to it, and you can add your own for anything particular to your premises.

Fire safety

Assessments, alarms, lighting and equipment.

  • Fire risk assessments
  • Fire alarm servicing
  • Emergency lighting
  • and more
Electrical

Installation and equipment testing.

  • Installation condition reports
  • Portable appliance testing
  • and more
Water hygiene

Keeping water systems safe.

  • Legionella risk assessments
  • Temperature monitoring
  • and more
Building and services

The wider fabric and plant.

  • Asbestos management
  • Gas and heating
  • Air conditioning
  • and more
Made for your premises

Add a title for anything specific to you, from kitchen extract cleaning to a particular piece of equipment.

These are examples. Which checks apply, and how often, depends on the premises, the equipment, current legislation and guidance, and your risk assessment. Nothing here is legal advice.
